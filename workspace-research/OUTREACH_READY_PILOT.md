# OUTREACH_READY_PILOT.md

## Purpose

Give the execution owner a ready-to-send pilot package for the top-ranked Feishu ops copilot offer.

## Offer to pitch

- Offer name: 5-day Feishu weekly status pilot
- Buyer: founder-led teams with 5-20 staff already using Feishu for daily coordination
- Pain: one manager or founder still spends time chasing weekly updates manually
- Outcome promised: fewer missed updates, less manual chasing, one clean weekly summary in Feishu
- Pilot scope: one workflow only for weekly status collection, reminders, summary generation, and one Feishu handoff
- Price: RMB 3,999 fixed-scope pilot
- Delivery window: 5 working days after access and workflow sign-off

## Best-first buyer profile

Prioritize teams that match all of the following:

- Feishu is already the default internal tool
- weekly status updates already happen, but manually
- founder, chief of staff, ops lead, or team lead feels the pain directly
- team is small enough to move fast without procurement drag
- current process depends on chasing people in chat or docs

Deprioritize or skip teams that match any of the following:

- they want a CRM, project-management rebuild, or custom app instead of one narrow workflow
- they do not already run weekly updates
- they barely use Feishu day to day
- decision-maker cannot approve a small pilot quickly
- the pain is vague and not tied to time lost or missed follow-up

## What the buyer gets

- one reminder cadence for weekly update collection
- one intake format for team updates
- one summary output written into Feishu
- one handoff destination such as a doc or chat thread
- one revision round after first live run

Not included:

- multi-team rollout
- custom app development
- historical data migration
- CRM replacement
- deep permissions cleanup

## Outreach angle

Lead with the operational outcome, not the agent stack.

Use this framing:

- your team stops chasing weekly updates manually
- the pilot is fixed-scope and low-risk
- delivery is fast and uses the tools they already have
- the result is a cleaner manager summary with less follow-up overhead

## Message templates

### Warm outreach

"Quick idea: I can set up one fixed-scope Feishu workflow in 5 working days so your team stops chasing weekly updates manually. The pilot covers reminders, status intake, summary generation, and a single doc/chat handoff. Fixed price is RMB 3,999. If your team already runs weekly updates in Feishu, want me to send the 1-page scope?"

### Short follow-up

"Following up in case this is useful: the pilot is only for one weekly status workflow, not a full system rebuild. If weekly chasing is still manual on your side, I can send the exact scope and example output."

### Referral ask

"If this is not for you, do you know a founder or ops lead using Feishu who is still manually chasing weekly updates? I am testing a narrow 5-day pilot and only need a few relevant conversations."

## Discovery call goals

In the first 20-30 minute call, confirm only these points:

- who currently chases updates
- how many people are involved
- how often updates are missed or late
- where updates live now in Feishu
- whether they would pay RMB 3,999 for a narrow pilot this month

## Qualification checklist

A prospect is qualified if most answers are yes:

- they already use Feishu daily
- they already run some form of weekly status collection
- one owner clearly feels the current pain
- they want one narrow workflow fixed first
- they can say yes to a small pilot without heavy internal process

## Concrete evidence to capture

Record these fields for every outreach attempt:

- company or team name
- contact role
- why they fit the buyer profile
- sent date
- reply status
- objection or interest signal
- call booked or not
- willingness to pay: yes, no, maybe, or counteroffer
- current weekly pain in time or missed updates

## Success threshold for this wave

The first outreach wave is good enough to validate if it produces:

- 3 positive replies from 10 targeted messages
- 1 live scoping call
- 1 prospect willing to pay or enter procurement for the RMB 3,999 pilot

## Handoff note for execution owner

Use the warm outreach script first with 5-10 founder-led teams that already use Feishu and visibly coordinate work in weekly cycles. Do not broaden the pitch until this narrow workflow either gets paid traction or clearly fails.
