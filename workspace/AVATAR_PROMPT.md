# AVATAR_PROMPT.md

## Goal

Create an original male avatar with a cool, clean, high-end look. Inspired by the feeling of a cold, handsome young male celebrity type, but not a copy of any real person.

## Prompt

A portrait of an original young East Asian man, early 20s, clean black hair, sharp bone structure, calm cool expression, elegant and restrained, high-end minimal styling, pale skin tone, refined jawline, straight nose, clear eyes, subtle distance, quiet charisma, luxury fashion editorial feeling, soft natural lighting, low saturation, cool gray and deep navy palette, cinematic composition, ultra clean background, premium polished details, realistic illustration, tasteful and modern, not smiling too much, cool and composed, handsome in a crisp and understated way

## Negative prompt

low quality, blurry, extra fingers, distorted face, cartoonish proportions, exaggerated smile, messy background, neon colors, cheesy idol poster style, copied celebrity face, watermark, text, logo

## Style notes

- Keep it original; do not imitate any real celebrity exactly
- Aim for clean, cold, refined, quietly attractive
- Better understated than overstyled
- Expression should feel controlled, intelligent, and slightly aloof

## Square avatar variant

Close-up portrait, centered face, shoulders visible, clean background, strong silhouette, optimized for profile picture crop
